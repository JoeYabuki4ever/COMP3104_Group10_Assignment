1. College overview
George Brown College, identified on its current official website as George
Brown Polytechnic, is a postsecondary institution in Toronto, Ontario.
It offers full-time, part-time and continuing education options leading to
credentials such as certificates, diplomas and degrees. Its location connects
students with the businesses and organizations operating in Toronto.

2. Campuses and programs
The official website lists three main campuses: Casa Loma, St. James and
Waterfront. Academic areas include applied science and engineering technology,
business and creative industries, culinary arts, community services and health
sciences. Students can explore different programs and delivery options through
the institution's online program resources.

3. Practical learning and industry connections
George Brown works with industry partners that contribute to curriculum,
field education and employment opportunities. Its work-integrated learning
approach connects academic study with experience in professional settings.
Depending on the program, this can include placements, co-ops, internships,
applied research or apprenticeships. These opportunities involve cooperation
between the student, the institution and a host organization. They connect
classroom knowledge with workplace expectations and practical responsibilities.

4. Student resources
The college website provides access to the Library Learning Commons, tutoring,
career services, counselling and Accessible Learning Services. Students also
use Brightspace (D2L) for course activities and STU-View for academic services.

5. Connection to this assignment
This group assignment is an example of learning through practice. Researching
information, organizing files and collaborating through Git helps students
develop technical and communication skills. Recording changes clearly also
helps a team explain what each member contributed to a shared project.
